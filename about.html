Professional profile

I am a foodgrain storage and depot operations professional with over 15 years of experience with the Food Corporation of India (FCI).

I joined FCI in April 2011 as Assistant Grade-III (Depot) and progressed through Assistant Grade-II to Assistant Grade-I (Depot). My career has brought together field-level operations and divisional administration.

I have handled depot-level responsibilities as Depot In-charge and served as Rail Head In-charge at Naini. My work spans receipt, storage and issue of foodgrains, procurement, inventory management, physical verification, and monitoring of storage loss and gain.

With a B.E. in Computer Science & Engineering, I apply my technical background to operational problems—developing practical tools to simplify calculations and improve efficiency.

Beyond work, I am a published Hindi poet and a cricket player representing FCI Prayagraj.
