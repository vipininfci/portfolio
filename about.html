Professional Profile

I am a foodgrain storage and depot operations professional with over 15 years of experience with the Food Corporation of India (FCI). I joined FCI in April 2011 as Assistant Grade-III (Depot) and progressed through Assistant Grade-II to Assistant Grade-I (Depot).

My career spans depot operations, procurement, administration, commercial work, vigilance & security, rail-head operations, inventory management, physical verification, and monitoring of storage loss and gain. With a B.E. in Computer Science & Engineering, I also apply technology to operational challenges.
